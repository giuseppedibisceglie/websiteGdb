Bio

Giuseppe Di Bisceglie is an Electronic Music student at the Conservatory Martini of Bologna.

Originally trained as a guitarist, exploring fingerstyle, fingerpicking, and harmony, he later expanded his musical language into electronic and electroacoustic composition.

Active both in the studio and live, he blends digital and analog instruments in live electronics performances and electroacoustic improvisation, both solo and in collaboration with other musicians.

He works daily with Ableton Live, Max/MSP, and Reaper for sound design and music production, with expertise in Ambisonics and multichannel composition.

Madeleine
2024
Synopsis
Madeleine intertwines heterogeneous acoustic materials, such as balloons, glockenspiel, and various toys, with evocative fragments of pop-rock tracks. The moments of clear recognizability of the sources, contrasted with electroacoustic reworkings of various kinds, aim to activate memory processes, allowing imagination to assemble a frosted mosaic of memories.
